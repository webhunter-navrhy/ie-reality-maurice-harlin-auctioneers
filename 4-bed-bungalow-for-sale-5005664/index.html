About the property

Description property

A Fabulous Country Home on c.0.80 acre at Ringlestown, Kilmessan, Co Meath A 4 Bedroom Detached Bungalow Residence, its origins was a Traditional Land Commission Cottage on 22 Acres, evolved into a spacious and comfortable Bungalow in 1970's on an elevated site enjoying great views from front terrace over surrounding countryside. A Mature Private Property 2 Km from Kilmessan Village (Supermarket, National School, Station House Hotel & Monto's Restaurants & A Butchers Shop, 6 minutes from M3 Motorway and about 15 minutes to Navan, Trim and Dunshaughlin, adjacent to Royal Tara Golf Club & Hill of Tara. Originally a working Farm, having lots of outbuildings, additional lands available by separate negotiation

Accommodation

Entrance hallway, spacious Living Room with an open fireplace, Sitting Room/Dining Room with two Windows facing west with great views over countryside, Fully fitted Solid Wood Kitchen with an everyday Dining area, rear hallway, 4 Double Bedrooms, a Family Bathroom and a Laundry Room.

Outside has a hardcore driveway, Car parking spaces for 10+ Cars, a Row of Outbuildings suitable for storage, garden or Hobby use, front side and rear gardens, mature trees and shrubs
